Laser Cutting – Custom Keychain

Bridging the gap between software design and the physical world through digital fabrication.

Project Description

As part of an individual hands-on activity, I successfully designed and created a custom keychain using state-of-the-art laser-cutting technology.

I utilized RDWorks V8 to develop the conceptual design, prepare the intricate artwork, and meticulously configure the speed and power parameters required for laser cutting. The prepared digital vector design was then transferred to the laser-cutting machine to precisely fabricate the physical keychain.

This hands-on activity provided immense practical exposure to the entire digital fabrication process. It allowed me to deeply understand exactly how a computer-generated 2D design is transformed into a tangible, physical product using subtractive manufacturing.

Key Skills Gained

  • Basic fundamental design creation and workspace familiarity using RDWorks V8.
  • Holistic understanding of the laser-cutting hardware workflow.
  • Preparing strict digital files mapping out cutting and engraving parameters for machine execution.
  • Converting a purely digital software design into a highly precise physical product.
  • Developing personal creativity, technical precision, and practical problem-solving skills.
