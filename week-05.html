Week 05: Mid-Phase Discoveries

Wrapping up Phase 01 with deep dives into prototyping and defining our core problem statement.

Week 05 marks the culmination of our very first phase in the PRICE Protosem journey. This week was entirely focused on narrowing down our problem statements and preparing our very first conceptual prototypes.

By blending our early research in Retail and Commerce with the technical possibilities of AI and Analytics, my team and I brainstormed multiple avenues. We faced interesting challenges trying to define exactly what the end-user truly needs—rather than what we assumed they wanted.

As we transition into Phase 02, I am incredibly excited to start breathing life into our concepts with some hands-on coding and deeper data modeling.
