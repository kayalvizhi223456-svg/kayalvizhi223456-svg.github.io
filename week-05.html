Week 05: Digital Fabrication Focus

Exploring subtractive and additive manufacturing through intensive individual hands-on activities.

Laser Cutting – Custom Keychain Design

RDWorks V8 Digital Fabrication & Laser Cutting Individual Hands-on Activity

As part of an individual hands-on activity, I designed and created a custom keychain using laser-cutting technology. I used RDWorks V8 to develop the design, prepare the artwork, and configure it for laser cutting. The digital design was then transferred to the laser-cutting machine to produce the physical keychain.

This activity provided practical exposure to the digital fabrication process, allowing me to understand how a computer-generated design can be transformed into a tangible product. Through this experience, I developed skills in design preparation, laser-cutting software, precision, and creative problem-solving.

Key Skills Gained

  • Basic design creation using RDWorks V8.
  • Understanding the laser-cutting workflow.
  • Preparing digital files for machine execution.
  • Converting a digital design into a physical product.
  • Developing creativity, precision, and practical technical skills.

3D Printing – Design and Fabrication

Bambu Studio Additive Manufacturing Individual Hands-on Activity

As part of an individual hands-on activity, I explored the process of 3D printing by preparing a digital model and transforming it into a physical object. Using Bambu Studio, I imported and positioned the 3D model, configured the necessary printing parameters, and generated the sliced file for fabrication.

This activity provided practical experience in understanding the additive manufacturing workflow, where objects are created layer by layer using 3D printing technology. It enhanced my knowledge of digital model preparation, slicing techniques, and the practical application of 3D printing in prototyping and product development.

Key Skills Gained

  • Basic operation of Bambu Studio.
  • 3D model preparation and positioning.
  • Understanding slicing and layer-by-layer printing.
  • Familiarity with 3D printing parameters.
  • Hands-on experience in digital fabrication.
  • Development of creativity, precision, and problem-solving skills.
